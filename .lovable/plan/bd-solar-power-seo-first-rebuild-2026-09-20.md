# BD Solar Power SEO-first rebuild

## Goal
Reposition the existing site around verified rooftop-solar installation, pricing, net metering, savings, and lead capture while retaining working tools, enquiry storage, analytics, and search-engine rendering.

## What will change
1. **Verified content source** — centralize business details, contact links, pricing, FAQs, locations, services, projects, policy notices, and page metadata. Unsupported claims will be removed or clearly marked as editable placeholders.
2. **Shared customer journey** — rebuild the main menu and footer, add the announcement bar, mobile Call/WhatsApp/Quote actions, breadcrumbs, and a reusable quote dialog connected to the existing enquiry flow.
3. **Homepage** — replace the mixed investor-platform message with the requested installation-first page, visible 12-question FAQ, system choices, process, calculator preview, net-metering guidance, project placeholders, locations, and final quote action.
4. **Core pages** — add the pricing hub, 1/3/5/10kW pages, net-metering guide, residential page, commercial hub and four sector pages, full calculator, projects gallery, five location pages, About, and Contact.
5. **Blog and internal links** — preserve existing articles, add category filtering and the six requested high-intent article templates without duplicating current topics, and connect articles to relevant services and quote pages.
6. **Search visibility** — give every public page unique metadata, breadcrumbs, canonical and social fields, visible FAQ markup where applicable, and appropriate Organization, LocalBusiness, Service, FAQ, and page data. Review data will remain absent until genuine reviews exist.
7. **Crawl and quality checks** — add all public routes to prerendering and the sitemap, then verify mobile/desktop layouts, keyboard use, calculator behavior, quote submission, metadata, internal links, and production output.

## Content safeguards
- Keep **5kW from BDT 275,000**, free survey, net-metering support, nationwide service, and 25-year panel warranty as supplied business statements.
- Present the Tk 10.50 incentive as conditional and place the required policy-verification disclaimer beside it.
- Treat all other prices, savings, generation, roof area, payback, credentials, project performance, installation counts, and response times as illustrative or editable until verified.
- Use placeholders for project proof, certificates, team photos, and local maps rather than fabricated evidence.

## Technical approach
- Preserve React, TypeScript, Tailwind, shadcn/ui, the current database and notification flow, GA4 tracking, and build-time prerendering.
- Use reusable templates for system sizes, commercial sectors, locations, and articles so future edits stay consistent.
- Keep existing authenticated tools and investor pages available but remove them from the primary customer navigation rather than deleting working functionality.
- Preserve the existing custom-domain Google Maps fallback until a user-owned domain-authorized key is connected.
